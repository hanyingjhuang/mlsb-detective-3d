# 醫檢偵探局 3D

A 3D detective game made for the Open Day of the Department of Medical Laboratory Science and Biotechnology, Taipei Medical University. Players solve three fictional cases (molecular forensics, hematology and clinical microbiology) through 19 evidence puzzles.

**Play:** https://hanyingjhuang.github.io/mlsb-detective-3d/

The game runs in any current browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) on a computer or tablet, with nothing to install. All stories, characters and test results are fictional and are meant for teaching only.
